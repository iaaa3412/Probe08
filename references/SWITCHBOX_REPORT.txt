 1.  WHY THIS DOCUMENT EXISTS
===============================================================================

There are three relay cards in each bench's HP 75000 Series B (E1300A)
mainframe. They look interchangeable from GPIB - all three answer *IDN? with
the identical string:

    HEWLETT-PACKARD,SWITCHBOX,0,A.06.00                             [MEASURED]

They are not interchangeable. Two different card families are in use, they
behave differently, and the same GPIB secondary address holds a DIFFERENT card
depending on which bench is plugged in. Closing "channel 3" without knowing
which card answered is how you energise something you did not intend.

The only reliable identification is SYST:CTYP?, per slot.                [MEASURED]


===============================================================================
 2.  WHAT IS FITTED, AND WHERE
===============================================================================

Both benches present one E1300A mainframe at GPIB primary address 9. Each
configured module appears at its own SECONDARY address.

                    probe02 (the LaMP bench)      probe03 (dev bench)
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  9::0              E1300A mainframe              E1300A mainframe
  9::3              E1326B multimeter  (LADDR 24) --
  9::7              --                            E1326B, FAILED (LADDR 56)
  9::9              E1343A mux         (LADDR 72) --
  9::10             --                            E1364A form C  (LADDR 80)
  9::14             E1364A form C     (LADDR 112) E1364A form C  (LADDR 112)
  9::15             E1345A mux        (LADDR 120) E1343A mux     (LADDR 120)
                                                                        [MEASURED]

  NOTE THE TRAP: 9::15 is an E1345A on probe02 and an E1343A on probe03.
  9::14 is a form C switch on both. probe03's only WIRED card is at 9::10,
  which does not exist on probe02 at all.

Card types in service:

  E1343A   16-channel HIGH VOLTAGE relay multiplexer   250 Vdc / 354 Vac pk  [MANUAL]
  E1345A   16-channel relay multiplexer                120 Vdc / 170 Vac pk  [MANUAL]
  E1364A   16-channel form C (SPDT) switch             250 V, 1 A, 30 W      [MANUAL]

The E1344A / E1347A thermocouple variants share the same manual but are not
fitted on either bench.


===============================================================================
 3.  HOW A CARD IS ADDRESSED
===============================================================================

Three nested levels. Getting any of them wrong silently talks to the wrong
thing.

    GPIB primary 9
      |
      +-- secondary address  =  VXI logical address / 8              [MEASURED]
      |     e.g. LADDR 120 -> secondary 15 -> GPIB0::9::15::INSTR
      |
      +-- card number within the switchbox (1..8)
      |     lowest logical address in the group is card 01           [MANUAL]
      |     every card here is alone in its group, so card = 1       [MEASURED]
      |
      +-- channel number 00..15  (plus 90/91/92 on multiplexers)

Channel list syntax:

    (@ccnn)     cc = card number, nn = channel      e.g. (@0103) or (@103)
                Leading zero on the card may be dropped: "close channel 02 of
                card 1, send 102 instead of 0102".                    [MANUAL]
                Both forms verified accepted.                       [MEASURED]

    Tree switches are addressed the same way: (@190) is card 1, channel 90.


===============================================================================
 4.  CARD FAMILY A - RELAY MULTIPLEXERS (E1343A / E1345A)
===============================================================================

Each channel carries THREE lines: High (H), Low (L), Guard (G).       [MANUAL]
Sixteen channels, split into two banks:

        Bank 0  =  channels 00 .. 07
        Bank 1  =  channels 08 .. 15                                  [MANUAL]

THE PART THAT CATCHES PEOPLE OUT: closing a channel connects it only to its
bank common. It reaches the outside world through a TREE SWITCH, and the tree
switch is itself an addressable channel. Close a channel without its tree
switch and you have connected nothing.

        channel 90  =  AT  tree switch
        channel 91  =  BT  tree switch
        channel 92  =  AT2 tree switch                                [MANUAL]
                        all three verified to close and read back   [MEASURED]

  BLOCK DIAGRAM
  -------------

                                                       ANALOG BUS CONNECTOR
    ch00 --+                                           (ribbon to the E1326B)
    ch01 --+                                                  |
     ...   +--- Bank 0 common ---[ AT  ch90 ]--- AT terms ----+--  H  L  G
    ch07 --+                          |                           (SENSE pair)
                                      |
                                 [ AT2 ch92 ]
                                      |
    ch08 --+                          |
    ch09 --+                          |
     ...   +--- Bank 1 common ---[ BT  ch91 ]--- BT terms ----+--  I+ I- IG
    ch15 --+                                                      (SOURCE pair)

    Every common line, tree switch line and analog bus line carries a
    100 ohm series resistor for relay protection.                     [MANUAL]
    That resistance IS in your 2-wire measurement. It does not matter at
    megohms; it matters a great deal at single ohms.

  WHAT EACH TREE SWITCH GIVES YOU
  -------------------------------

    ch90  AT    Bank 0 channels  ->  AT terminals  AND  analog bus H / L / G
    ch91  BT    Bank 1 channels  ->  BT terminals  AND  analog bus I+ / I- / IG
    ch92  AT2   Bank 1 channels  ->  AT terminals                     [MANUAL]

  So the analog bus splits the card in half: Bank 0 is the SENSE side, Bank 1
  is the SOURCE side. That is exactly the pairing the multimeter expects, and
  it is why 4-wire scanning pairs channel N with channel N+8.

  SELECTING ONE DEVICE, 2-WIRE
  ----------------------------

      ROUT:CLOS (@190)        close the AT tree switch
      ROUT:CLOS (@103)        close channel 03  (must be 00..07)

      device on channel 03  --H/L-->  Bank 0 common  -->  AT terminals
                                                     -->  analog bus H/L

  SELECTING ONE DEVICE, 4-WIRE (KELVIN)
  -------------------------------------

      ROUT:CLOS (@190)        AT tree  - sense path
      ROUT:CLOS (@191)        BT tree  - source path
      ROUT:CLOS (@103)        sense channel  N        (Bank 0)
      ROUT:CLOS (@111)        source channel N+8      (Bank 1)

      N and N+8 must be the same device, wired to both channels. This is the
      pairing SCAN:MODE FRES uses.                                    [MANUAL]
      All four closures verified accepted together.                 [MEASURED]


===============================================================================
 5.  CARD FAMILY B - FORM C SWITCH (E1364A)
===============================================================================

Completely different animal. Sixteen INDEPENDENT single-pole double-throw
relays. No banks, no tree switches, no analog bus.                    [MANUAL]

Each channel has three terminals:

        C    Common   - the moving contact
        NO   Normally Open
        NC   Normally Closed

                    NC  o------.
                               |
        C  o--------- /        +-- relay coil energised = C to NO
                       \       |   de-energised         = C to NC
                    NO  o------'

        CLOSE the channel  ->  C connects to NO                       [MANUAL]
        OPEN  the channel  ->  C connects to NC                       [MANUAL]
        A reset connects every C to its NC.                           [MANUAL]

  *** THE RELAYS ARE LATCHING ***                                     [MANUAL]

    "the relay remains in the last state during power-up or power-down"

    The card comes back from a power cycle holding whatever it was left in.
    All-open is the POST-RESET state, not the power-on state. Never assume
    the state of a form C card you have not just reset.

  RATINGS, per channel                                                [MANUAL]

    250 Vdc or 250 Vac peak (177 Vac RMS)
    1 A dc or ac RMS, non-inductive
    30 W or 40 VA maximum
    ~15 ms closure time  ->  ceiling of roughly 50 Hz scanning

  All 16 channels on probe03's wired card were exercised individually and
  read back correctly, 55 ms per close/read/open/read cycle.         [MEASURED]

 6.  COMMAND REFERENCE
===============================================================================

  Everything below was sent to the live cards unless marked otherwise.

  ROUTING
    ROUT:CLOS (@1nn)          close channel nn                      [MEASURED]
    ROUT:OPEN (@1nn)          open it                               [MEASURED]
    ROUT:CLOS? (@1nn)         -> "1" closed, "0" open                [MEASURED]
    *RST                      open EVERY channel on the card        [MEASURED]

    CLOS/OPEN also accept the bare form: CLOS (@1nn) without ROUT:  [MEASURED]

  IDENTITY AND STATE
    *IDN?                     -> HEWLETT-PACKARD,SWITCHBOX,0,A.06.00
                                 identical on all three cards       [MEASURED]
    SYST:CTYP? <slot>         -> the actual card type. THE ONLY WAY
                                 to tell the cards apart.           [MEASURED]
    SYST:ERR?                 -> one error, clearing as it goes     [MEASURED]
    SYST:VERS?                -> 1990.0  (SCPI version)             [MEASURED]

  SCANNING
    ROUT:SCAN:PORT ABUS       route scanned channels to analog bus  [MEASURED]
    ROUT:SCAN:PORT NONE       default; no analog bus routing        [MEASURED]
    ROUT:SCAN:PORT?           -> ABUS | NONE                        [MEASURED]
    TRIG:SOUR?                -> IMM at reset                       [MEASURED]
    INIT:CONT?                -> 0 at reset                         [MEASURED]

  NOT SUPPORTED on this firmware - do not waste time on them:
    ROUT:SCAN?                -> -113 "Undefined header"            [MEASURED]
    ROUT:FUNC?                -> -109 "Missing parameter"           [MEASURED]

  MAINFRAME (secondary address 0, not the cards)
    VXI:CONF:NUMB?            how many modules on the backplane     [MEASURED]
    VXI:CONF:DLAD?            their logical addresses               [MEASURED]
    VXI:READ? <laddr>,<off>   read a module register                [MEASURED]
    VXI:WRITE <laddr>,<off>,<val>   write one  -- SEE SECTION 10    [MEASURED]

  VXI:CONF:DLAD? is the authoritative inventory. A module that is present but
  unassigned appears there and NOWHERE else - VISA will not even open its
  address. A blind address sweep cannot find it.                     [MEASURED]

 7.  WIRING AN ACTUAL MEASUREMENT
===============================================================================

  The test being reproduced (from the .PMA recipes and LaMP's own tables):
  apply 10 V, measure leakage current, to verify electrical isolation.
  Recipe parameters: Voltage=10, MeterRange=1e-4, MeterCurrentLimit=1e-6,
  NPLC=1.                                                            [MEASURED]

  OPTION A - Keithley 2400 through a multiplexer   (recommended)
  -------------------------------------------------------------
  One instrument sources and measures. It is what LaMP used
  (fldIsLampElectrical = TRUE, alongside RELAY1 and the 2001X).

      2400 FORCE HI ------> AT tree switch terminal  H
      2400 FORCE LO ------> AT tree switch terminal  L

      per die N (N = 0..7):
          ROUT:CLOS (@190)          AT tree
          ROUT:CLOS (@10N)          the die's channel
          :SOUR:FUNC VOLT ; :SOUR:VOLT 10
          :SENS:FUNC 'CURR' ; :SENS:CURR:PROT 1E-6
          :OUTP ON ; :READ?

      Sixteen channels = up to 16 two-terminal devices per card, selected one
      at a time. Far cleaner than the form C arrangement in section 8.

  OPTION B - E1326B over the analog bus
  -------------------------------------
      The multiplexer's analog bus connector cables straight to the E1326B,
      no external wiring. Bank 0 -> sense, Bank 1 -> source.
      *** On probe02 this cable is NOT FITTED. See section 10. ***  [MEASURED]

      E1326B ceiling is 1.048 Mohm, which an isolation reading will exceed.

  OPTION C - HP 3458A
  -------------------
      Its ohms functions SOURCE a known current and read the resulting
      voltage. Ranges to 1.2 Gohm - about a thousand times the E1326B's
      headroom, and the right tool if you want resistance rather than
      leakage-at-10-V.                                                [MANUAL]

          range      current sourced        range      current sourced
          10 ohm         10 mA              1 Mohm         5 uA
          100 ohm         1 mA              10 Mohm      500 nA
          1 kohm          1 mA              100 Mohm     500 nA
          10 kohm       100 uA              1 Gohm       500 nA
          100 kohm       50 uA

      It cannot be commanded to 10 V - it is a current source. If the bias
      voltage matters for comparability with LaMP's data, use option A.

      Its front/rear Terminals switch is MECHANICAL and cannot be set over
      GPIB; TERM? reports it.  probe02 reads REAR, probe03 reads FRONT. [MEASURED]


===============================================================================
 8.  probe03's FORM C WIRING (THE 2x2 DIE MAP)
===============================================================================

  Transcribed in references/probe03mapping, encoded in
  instruments/hp_switchbox.py. Card is the E1364A at 9::10.

  The prober lands on a 2x2 shot; the relays then select each of the four dies
  in turn, two pins per die, eight coax in total.

    die   channels      coax        lands on
    -------------------------------------------------------
     1    CH00 CH01     3, 4        Input HI  / Input LO
     2    CH02 CH03     5, 6        Input HI  / Input LO
     3    CH08 CH09     7, 8        Current HI / Current LO
     4    CH10 CH11     9, 10       Current HI / Current LO

    HI-side channels: 00, 02, 08, 10   (coax 3, 5, 7, 9)
    LO-side channels: 01, 03, 09, 11   (coax 4, 6, 8, 10)
    Channels 04-07 and 12-14 are unwired spares.

  EVERY NC TERMINAL IS DAISY-CHAINED TO GROUND via CH15 NC. So an OPEN channel
  GROUNDS its probe pin. All-open is a guarded state, not a floating one:

      CH00 NC - CH01 NC - CH02 NC - CH03 NC - CH11 NC - CH10 NC
               - CH09 NC - CH08 NC - CH15 NC ---> ground

  OPERATING RULE: one HI channel and one LO channel closed at a time.
  Two HI closed at once shorts two probe pins through the HI terminal.
  instruments/hp_switchbox.py refuses this rather than trusting the caller.

  UNRESOLVED for this bench: two pins per die means the current has to be
  forced down the same pair the voltage is read from, so Input HI must be
  commoned with Current HI (and Input LO with Current LO). probe03mapping
  records no such strap. Without it, dies 1-2 can only be sensed and dies 3-4
  only driven, and no die is measurable.                              [INFERRED]
  One continuity check at the adapter settles it.


===============================================================================
 9.  WHAT IS NOT ESTABLISHED
===============================================================================

  - Which physical die of the 2x2 quad is quad position 1, 2, 3 or 4.
    The device IDs show positions 1 and 3 share a prefix and 2 and 4 share
    another, so 1,3 are one row and 2,4 the other - but which row is upper is
    not determined by any data seen so far. This blocks tying relay switch N
    to a physical die.                                                [INFERRED]

  - Whether probe02's multiplexer channels are wired to a probe card at all,
    and if so in what order. Nothing was traced on that bench.

  - The strap question in section 8.

  - Whether LaMP used 2-wire or 4-wire. Its recipes give a current LIMIT
    (1 uA) and a meter RANGE (100 uA) but not a wiring mode.


===============================================================================
 10. WAYS TO FOOL YOURSELF  (READ THIS ONE)
===============================================================================

  (a) A FLOATING INPUT DRIFTS, AND THE DRIFT LOOKS LIKE SWITCHING.

      Measuring the E1326B while changing multiplexer state produced readings
      that changed every time - apparently proving the analog bus worked.
      A control run of the SAME state, repeated, gave:

          6516 -> 8337 -> 8203 -> 7161 -> 5834 ohm

      and interleaving two states decayed straight through both, into
      NEGATIVE resistance. It was the meter's input settling with nothing
      connected. The first reading after a reset was 9.9e37 - overload, i.e.
      open, the honest answer.

      The analog bus cable is NOT fitted on probe02.                 [MEASURED]

      ALWAYS run the repeat-the-same-state control before believing that a
      relay changed anything.

  (b) A CHANNEL WITHOUT ITS TREE SWITCH CONNECTS NOTHING.
      Section 4. Easy to miss; produces a clean, confident open circuit.

  (c) FORM C RELAYS LATCH.
      Section 5. The card powers up however it was left. Send *RST before
      trusting any state.

  (d) ALL THREE CARDS ANSWER *IDN? IDENTICALLY.
      Only SYST:CTYP? distinguishes them, and the mapping from address to
      card type differs between benches.

  (e) DO NOT WRITE 0 TO A FAILED MODULE'S CONTROL REGISTER.
      On probe03, VXI:WRITE to the failed E1326B's control register bit 1
      restored its ability to drive SYSFAIL. The Resource Manager had
      disabled that deliberately to hold the failed card off-line. The whole
      mainframe hung, and being mid-chain on GPIB it took the prober and the
      3458A down with it. Recovery was a power cycle.                [MEASURED]

  (f) A COLD GPIB OPEN CAN FAULT INSIDE THE VENDOR DLL.
      The first open of an address needs longer than 500 ms and fails with an
      access violation rather than a clean error; afterwards 500 ms is fine
      forever. instruments/gpib_base.py now retries at 2500 ms.       [MEASURED]

 11. QUICK REFERENCE CARD
===============================================================================

  IDENTIFY WHAT YOU ARE TALKING TO
      SYST:CTYP? 1

  SAFE STATE
      *RST                              all channels open

  MULTIPLEXER, 2-WIRE, device on channel N (N = 00..07)
      ROUT:CLOS (@190)                  AT tree switch
      ROUT:CLOS (@1NN)                  the channel

  MULTIPLEXER, 4-WIRE, device on channels N and N+8
      ROUT:CLOS (@190)                  AT tree - sense
      ROUT:CLOS (@191)                  BT tree - source
      ROUT:CLOS (@1NN)
      ROUT:CLOS (@1MM)                  MM = NN + 8

  FORM C, one die on probe03
      ROUT:CLOS (@1HH)                  one HI channel: 00 02 08 10
      ROUT:CLOS (@1LL)                  one LO channel: 01 03 09 11
      never two on the same side

  CHECK
      ROUT:CLOS? (@1nn)                 -> 1 or 0
      SYST:ERR?                         -> +0,"No error" when clean

  INVENTORY THE MAINFRAME
      VXI:CONF:NUMB?    /    VXI:CONF:DLAD?      (at GPIB0::9::0)
      or run references/find_vxi_instruments.py
